Product Details:
Product Name: Methotrexate 2.5 mg Tablets
Product Code: MTX-2.5-TAB
Batch/Lot Number: MTX250-994A
Expiry Date: 12/2027
Quantity Affected: 14 bottles (100 tablets/bottle)

Event Description:
We are submitting an urgent pharmacovigilance report regarding unexpected severe adverse reactions observed in three separate patients receiving the MTX-2.5-TAB formulation from batch MTX250-994A.

Within 48 hours of administration, patients exhibited severe rash, uncontrollable nausea, and elevated liver enzymes beyond the expected threshold for this dosage. Upon physical inspection of the remaining tablets in the affected bottles, our pharmacy staff noted abnormal black specks embedded within the tablets, suggesting potential particulate contamination during manufacturing.

Immediate Actions Taken:
1. Administration of this batch has been immediately halted across all our facilities.
2. The remaining 14 bottles from this batch have been quarantined in our secure holding area.
3. Patients are currently being treated for the adverse reactions and are in stable condition.

We urgently request an investigation into this batch, instruction for the return of the quarantined bottles for your analysis, and confirmation of whether a broader recall will be initiated. Please treat this as a high-priority patient safety issue.
